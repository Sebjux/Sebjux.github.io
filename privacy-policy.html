Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit or use LumenBooks, an online English reading library.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. Who operates LumenBooks?

LumenBooks is operated by Juraj Šuda (“we”, “us” or “our”).

  • Website: https://www.lumenbooks.org
  • Contact email: contact@lumenbooks.org

Where applicable data protection law requires a data controller, the operator identified above is the controller for the processing described in this policy.

2. Information processed

Information stored in your browser. LumenBooks may use browser local storage to remember reading progress, books marked as read or in progress, last-read position, and vocabulary or other preferences. This information is stored on your device by your browser and is not necessarily sent to our servers. You can remove it by clearing the website data in your browser. If the site changes how this information is stored, this policy will be updated.

Technical and security information. When the website is requested, our hosting, content delivery and security providers may process technical data such as IP address, request time, requested URL, browser and device information, and security or diagnostic logs. The exact data and retention periods depend on the services and settings in use.

Information you send us. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.

3. How we use information

Information may be used to provide and maintain the website, remember reading preferences on your device, respond to enquiries, protect the website against abuse, diagnose technical problems, comply with legal obligations, and — where enabled and legally permitted — display and measure advertising.

4. Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the legal basis appropriate to each activity. This may include our legitimate interests in operating and securing the website, taking steps at your request when responding to an enquiry, compliance with legal obligations, and your consent where consent is required, including for certain non-essential storage technologies and advertising activities.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

LumenBooks uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners use cookies, device identifiers, IP addresses or similar technologies to serve, limit, measure and protect advertisements, as well as to prevent fraud and detect invalid traffic. Depending on your location and browser settings, advertisements may be personalised or non-personalised.

Currently, LumenBooks does not have an integrated Consent Management Platform (CMP). As a result, advertising scripts load directly when pages containing ad slots are visited. We are working toward integrating a Google-certified CMP to allow European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and other users to granularly configure their advertising preferences. In the meantime, you can manage ad personalisation and tracking through your Google account settings and browser controls.

Learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: How Google uses information. You can review Google’s privacy information at Google Privacy Policy and advertising controls at Google Ads Settings.

6. Third-party services

LumenBooks relies on the following third-party providers to operate and deliver learning features:

  • Cloudflare: provides DNS management, content delivery network (CDN) distribution, and DDoS/security protection for our domain. When you access LumenBooks, traffic routes through Cloudflare, which processes technical connection data including IP addresses for security and performance purposes. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense: provides advertising delivery and measurement across the website. See the Google links in the advertising section above.
  • Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev): provides English phonetics and definitions when users look up words in the reader. Words submitted for lookup are sent to this external service (https://api.dictionaryapi.dev), and technical request information, such as your IP address, is processed to return definition data.
  • MyMemory Translation: provides vocabulary translation into selected target languages. Words submitted for translation are sent to the MyMemory translation service. See MyMemory.

7. Recipients and international transfers

Information may be processed by service providers acting on our behalf, or by third parties acting under their own privacy terms (for example, advertising providers). Some providers may process information in countries outside the European Economic Area. Where the GDPR applies, international transfers must use an appropriate transfer mechanism and safeguards where required by law.

8. Retention

Reading preferences stored locally remain in your browser until you delete them or they are overwritten. Emails are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the enquiry and maintain appropriate records. Technical logs and third-party data are retained according to the relevant provider’s settings, policies and applicable legal requirements.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location and the law that applies, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority. To make a request, contact us using the details above. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. Children

LumenBooks provides English reading and learning content. The website is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so that we can assess and address the request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, providers or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact contact@lumenbooks.org.
