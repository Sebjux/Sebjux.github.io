About LumenBooks

A digital library purposefully crafted to help English language learners build comprehension, expand vocabulary, and master authentic reading skills through graded stories.

Who LumenBooks Is For

LumenBooks is an interactive digital reading library designed for English language learners of all backgrounds. If you want to bridge the gap between classroom theory and real-world fluency, our extensive library offers captivating stories that make reading English engaging and practical.

CEFR Reading Levels (A1–C2)

Every story is calibrated according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). Whether you are a beginner taking your first steps at A1–A2, an intermediate reader consolidating grammar at B1–B2, or an advanced learner targeting nuanced mastery at C1–C2, you can easily filter and choose books that perfectly match your skill level.

Integrated Dictionary & Translations

Never let difficult words disrupt your reading momentum. Clicking or hovering over any unfamiliar word or phrase opens an instant popover with comprehensive definitions, IPA phonetics, and contextual translations in your preferred native language.

Personal Vocabulary Collection

Build your active vocabulary effortlessly as you read. Mark words as learning with a single click to save them into your personal vocabulary drawer, where you can organize, review, and practice them anytime with smart flashcards.

Natural Learning Through Stories

Extensive reading is one of the most effective methods to acquire a second language. By immersing yourself in diverse genres—from sci-fi adventures and business insights to timeless classics and daily dialogues—you absorb vocabulary, sentence structures, and idioms naturally in context, building long-term reading confidence.
