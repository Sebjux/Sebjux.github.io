Cookie Policy

This page explains how LumenBooks may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Implementation note: This is a policy template, not a live cookie scanner or consent mechanism. Before publishing, check the technologies actually set by your website and CMP, and add a working way for visitors to reopen or change their consent choices.

1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small files stored by a website in your browser. Similar technologies, including local storage, can store information on your device or access information already stored there. These technologies may support website functionality, remember preferences, provide security or support advertising.

2. Local storage on LumenBooks

LumenBooks may use your browser’s local storage to remember reading-related information, such as books you have started or completed, your last reading position and vocabulary preferences. This information is stored in your browser and is intended to make the reading experience more convenient. It may remain on your device until you clear the website data or the application changes or removes it.

Local storage is not the same thing as a cookie. However, laws in some jurisdictions regulate both cookies and similar technologies used to store or access information on a user’s device.

3. Advertising technologies

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to display and measure advertisements, limit repeated displays, detect invalid traffic and, depending on your settings and choices, personalise advertising.

More information is available in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Consent and your choices

Where applicable law requires consent before non-essential cookies or similar technologies are used, LumenBooks should request your choice through its consent-management interface before those technologies are activated. The choices available may depend on your location and the services enabled on the website.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time using the cookie settings control on the website, where available. Before publishing: connect this statement to the actual Google-certified CMP or other consent-management platform you use. Add a visible “Cookie Settings” link to the site footer and wire it to the CMP’s documented preference centre function. Do not leave a non-working link.

5. Browser controls

Most browsers allow you to view, block or delete cookies and clear website data. If you block certain technologies, some website features may not work as expected. Clearing browser storage may also remove your saved reading progress and vocabulary preferences.

6. Third-party services

Depending on the features enabled, third-party providers such as Cloudflare, Google (including AdSense and consent tools) and the MyMemory translation service may process technical information or use their own technologies. Their practices are governed by their respective privacy notices. The live website should be checked so this list accurately reflects the services in use.
