Cookie Policy

This page explains how LumenBooks may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small files stored by a website in your browser. Similar technologies, including local storage, can store information on your device or access information already stored there. These technologies may support website functionality, remember preferences, provide security or support advertising.

2. Local storage on LumenBooks

LumenBooks may use your browser’s local storage to remember reading-related information, such as books you have started or completed, your last reading position and vocabulary preferences. This information is stored in your browser and is intended to make the reading experience more convenient. It may remain on your device until you clear the website data or the application changes or removes it.

Local storage is not the same thing as a cookie. However, laws in some jurisdictions regulate both cookies and similar technologies used to store or access information on a user’s device.

3. Advertising technologies

LumenBooks uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure advertisements, limit frequency of ads, combat fraud, and personalise advertising according to user settings.

More information is available in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Consent and your choices

Currently, LumenBooks does not have an integrated Consent Management Platform (CMP). As a result, advertising scripts and related technologies load directly upon visiting pages that contain advertisements.

We are actively preparing the website for integration with a Google-certified Consent Management Platform. Once integrated, visitors will be able to manage their consent choices and preferences directly through a consent banner and a persistent Cookie Settings control. In the meantime, you can manage or block cookies and tracking technologies through your browser settings or via Google’s ad settings controls.

5. Browser controls

Most browsers allow you to view, block or delete cookies and clear website data. If you block certain technologies, some website features may not work as expected. Clearing browser storage may also remove your saved reading progress and vocabulary preferences.

6. Third-party services

The following external providers are utilized on LumenBooks and may process technical data or set cookies/identifiers in accordance with their own policies:

  • Cloudflare: Provides DNS, CDN, and DDoS protection for LumenBooks. Requests to LumenBooks route through Cloudflare's network, which processes IP addresses and security headers. See Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense: Uses cookies and identifiers to display, target, and measure ads on our reading pages. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Dictionary API (api.dictionaryapi.dev): Provides phonetic transcriptions and word definitions for the reader lookup feature. When you look up a word, a query is sent to https://api.dictionaryapi.dev along with technical connection details like your IP address.
  • MyMemory Translation: Provides English-to-target-language translations for saved vocabulary. Submitted terms are sent to MyMemory.

7. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this page when the website’s technologies, providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published here with a revised update date.

8. Contact
