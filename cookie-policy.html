Cookie Policy

This page explains which cookies, local storage and similar technologies LumenBooks uses, and how to change your choices.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, can store information on your device or read information already stored there. They can make a website work, remember your preferences, keep it secure or support advertising.

2. What LumenBooks uses

TechnologyPurposeType
Local storage (readingStatus, readingProgress)Remembers which books you started or finished and where you stopped readingFunctional, stored on your device
Local storage (lumen_vocab_v1, lumen_known_v1)Remembers words you saved or marked as knownFunctional, stored on your device
Local storage (lumen_lang_v1, lumen_font_scale)Remembers your translation language and text sizeFunctional, stored on your device
Google AdSense cookies and identifiersShow, limit and measure ads, detect invalid traffic and, with your consent, personalise adsAdvertising, set by Google and its partners
Google consent cookiesRemember your consent choicesNecessary for consent management

Local storage is not the same as a cookie, but some laws regulate both. Functional local storage on LumenBooks is used only to provide features you use and stays on your device until you clear your browser data.

3. Advertising technologies

Google AdSense and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to display and measure ads, limit how often you see an ad, detect invalid traffic and, depending on your settings and choices, personalise advertising. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage personalised ads in Google Ads Settings.

4. Consent and your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, advertising cookies are used only after you have given consent through our consent message. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the button below.

The consent message is not available right now (it may be blocked by your browser or an extension). You can still manage ads at Google Ads Settings or delete cookies in your browser settings.

5. Browser controls

Most browsers let you view, block or delete cookies and clear website data. If you block certain technologies, some features may not work as expected. Clearing browser storage also removes your saved reading progress and vocabulary.

6. Third-party services

Third-party providers may process technical information or use their own technologies. Their practices are governed by their own privacy notices:

  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy (AdSense, consent tools, Google Fonts)
  • MyMemory (word translation)

7. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this page when the website’s technologies, providers or legal requirements change. The latest version is always published here with a revised date.

8. Contact

For questions about cookies or similar technologies on LumenBooks, contact contact@lumenbooks.org.
